## 5. Findings

**Blocking. These are false capability claims in a list headed "Everything in this list works in the 1 October build":**

1. **Q1 (Medium): "full keyboard control" is not true.** `content/DEVELOPMENT.json:24` says "Reduced motion and full keyboard control are built in." The game binds camera look, orbit, zoom into first person and aiming only to the mouse:
   - `unity/AthenHill/Assets/AthenHill/Data/Controls.inputactions`: `Look` = `<Mouse>/delta`, `Orbit` = `<Mouse>/leftButton`, `Zoom` = `<Mouse>/scroll/y`, `Aim` = `<Mouse>/rightButton`.
   - `Scripts/GameInput.cs:16–18`.
   - `unity/README.md` Controls table.

   Keyboard access exists for movement, interaction and UI only.

   **Fix:** change that string to "Save and Continue. Reduced motion is built in, and menus and panels work from the keyboard (camera look, zoom and aiming still use the mouse)."
2. **Q2 (Medium): "Watch the day turn to night" overstates the build.** In normal play the clock starts **paused at 17:00** and does not advance:
   - `Art/Atmosphere/Dustbowl/WardDustbowl.asset:16–17` has `defaultHour: 17` and `startPaused: 1`.
   - `unity/README.md` says "the clock starts paused at 17:00".
   - `CityTimeOfDay.cs:96`.

   Time only changes through the developer time menu (`DeveloperTimeMenu.cs`, compiled for `UNITY_EDITOR || DEBUG` only) or the dev bridge. The night captures were set to 20:50 by the lookbook tool.

   **Fix:** change `content/DEVELOPMENT.json:23` to "See Ward by day and by night: lamps, signs and terminals switch on with the city's light clock (for now the time of day is set from a developer menu), with music, ambience and spatial sound."

**Fix in the same content pass. These are factual errors, Low:**

3. **Q3: the West Gate pair describes the wrong gate.** The concept (`art/west_gate_20260926/concept`) was a generated reference for the **−X exit to the Outer Berms**. Its README says it "guided the lintel sign, knee braces and pier hazard wraps. It is not a target render". The paired capture shows that exit. The pair text instead describes the 1 Oct timber gates on the **+X spawn arches** (`content/DEVELOPMENT.json:69`). The concept caption, "The build kept its own Meshy arches and added gates rather than copying this layout", makes the same mix-up (`assets/tools/selection.json:500` → `assets/manifest.json:2211`).

   Separately, "working gates" (`DEVELOPMENT.json:45`) and "closed with working timber gates" (`selection.json:196` → `manifest.json:824`) imply gates that open. AGENTS §3 says one box collider seals each opening.

   **Fixes:**
   - pair body: "The concept for the way out of Ward to the Outer Berms. The build took its stencilled lintel sign, knee braces and hazard-striped piers."
   - concept caption: "Generated reference for the West Gate exit to the Outer Berms (26 Sep). It guided the lintel sign, knee braces and hazard-striped piers."
   - timeline: replace "working gates" with "timber gates on the spawn arches"
   - apron caption: replace "closed with working timber gates" with "closed with braced timber gates"

   Then rerun `assets/tools/build_assets.py` and the build.
4. **Q4: the Karaveen pair implies the pottery stall was built from the sheet.** The 10 Sep sheet was built to measure as a placeholder stall. `art/karaveen_market_20260926/README.md` records that it was **disabled and replaced by hand-built stalls** on 26 Sep. "On the right" is also wrong at tablet and mobile widths, where the images stack.

   **Fix** for `DEVELOPMENT.json:73`: "Stalls were first drawn as front, side and back sheets, and one was built to measure from them; the market's stalls were later rebuilt by hand. Alongside, a pottery stall in the market today."
5. **Q5: the explanation for the mid-September gap is unsupported.** `DEVELOPMENT.json:27` says "The quiet stretch in mid-September was planning and asset work that landed later." The project's own record is `unity/REFERENCE_STREET_PAUSE_20260910.md`, which reads "safe stop … Do not resume art production until the user asks". No project file changed between 12 and 24 Sep.

   **Fix:** "Dated from the project's own records. Work paused from 11 to 25 September."
6. **Q6: the Survive & Build status rail now contradicts Early testing.** `content/WEBSITE_COPY.md:372–375` still badges "Implants [PLANNED]" and "Five-tab equipment screen and armour [PLANNED]". The edited Early testing chapter and `/development` say the build has implant and armour slots.

   **Fix:** "Early character sheet: attributes, skills, implant and armour slots, loadouts, carry weight `[IN BUILD]` · Full six-attribute trickle-down system, eight implant locations and five-tab equipment screen `[PLANNED]`".
