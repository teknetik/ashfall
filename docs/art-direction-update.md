# Character and asset direction — 2026-09-07

The user explicitly rejected the primitive-looking player and supplied a
side-by-side comparison with `concept_art/main_male_human_player_character.jpg`.
That image is now the player's governing appearance reference: realistic
adult male anatomy and face, swept hair and full beard, layered worn olive
armour, dark flexible under-suit, practical pouches and small cyan signals.
The earlier simplified jacket interpretation does not meet this direction.

The user authorizes any external resources needed, primarily free, and is
willing to consider inexpensive paid resources. This overrides the earlier
no-external-assets rule. Check actual asset licences and retain source credits.
Prepare a concrete preview and current price before any paid purchase.

Use Blender to fit and adapt sourced meshes. Preserve controller/collision
behaviour and independently test animation, materials, scale and performance.
Judge the replacement from a close-up in the actual game beside the supplied
reference. Passing TypeScript, export or frame-rate checks is not visual
acceptance. Keep existing phase evidence as historical evidence.

Character replacement is the immediate priority. Phase 5 implementation may
finish its bounded QA in parallel; further system expansion is not a priority.
