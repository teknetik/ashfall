# Native integration 01 — independent static review

9 September 2026. Reviewer: `visual_critic`, separate from implementation. **Reject visual qualification in this build.** The replacement tree has much stronger biological branching and bark flow, but its installed root area has a hard intersection defect and its leaves remain below target. Character surface recovery does not resolve the visible source-quality limitations. No animation, temporal, night-lighting, route or performance acceptance is issued from these stills.

## Evidence and limits

Inspected all 25 named PNG views in [native evidence](../integration-01/native), including matched hill/avenue/hero, four root quadrants, bark and canopy close-ups, Vex, Finery, hall, shops, gates and mission terminals. The separate 60-view building audit is not included in this report. [Build record](../integration-01/build-record.json): built `2026-09-09T02:30:45.2114940Z`, scene SHA-256 `e3821054d7778aec016114aee67ef26b0a782a1063f925b4aa13e441ae01c9c6`, source record SHA-256 `011ef0dc54b59dd55b0f56a892b717d3af8c99a093bcdf03b07cc1846e683cf8`, base head `d4b867bb22a1489b5cd05373221b55dbec7bd5d4`. Build succeeded with zero errors and one warning; that is not art acceptance.

Recorded native Linux/OpenGLCore target: 1920×1080, render scale 1, MSAA 4, full texture setting, 4096 shadow map, 18 m shadow distance, RTX 3060/i9-10850K, all nine actors. The clock was paused at noon. These are static captures from a development build, not continuous gameplay. Snapshot FPS values are not a representative traversal benchmark. New lamp and platform-terminal families were **not installed** in this build.

The comparison uses the prior [independent baseline](../../20260908/reviews/baseline.md) and its actual Phase1 native evidence, plus the [accepted Ward target](../../../../../refs/courtyard_20260908/accepted-target.png). The directly observed official [Bethesda Akila overview/vendor panels](https://bethesda.net/en-US/news/guide-to-starfields-major-cities) remain a composition/construction benchmark, not a matched microtexture or animation test. The official Starfield Direct video was identified previously but no specific continuous sequence has been visually scored. No whole-game Bethesda parity claim is supported.
