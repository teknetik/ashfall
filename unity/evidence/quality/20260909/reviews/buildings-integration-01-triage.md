## Evidence coverage corrections

Most `side_left` and `side_right` contact panels are front-corner obliques. They are useful for the doorway approach, but do not reveal the complete side elevations or all service connections. Several back cameras tilt upward and crop the wall-footing contact. The roof views give useful silhouette/top coverage, but tiny roof details need a closer follow-up if reachable or clearly visible during play. Basic General's actor overlap obscures the shop-front counter area.

Before any building family passes, show each accessible full side, rear lower wall and footing, threshold, underside/attachments of the awning, and relevant roof edges in sun and shade at player height. Use an actor for scale without covering the defect under review, then provide a moving real-input route around the building. Keep the original evidence intact and add the missing angles; do not change unreviewed labels to accepted based on image counts.
