# Compact native motion review matrix — 26 September 2026

This is a review plan, not a record of completed tests. Static evidence has shown merged shaded leaf clusters, soft bark, stiff triangular grass, repetitive coarse paving, and comparable broad tree shadows between the two shadow-geometry candidates. **Shimmer, popping, sliding feet and jumping/turning faults have not been established by these stills.** The matrix tests those uncertainties without converting them into invented findings.

| Sequence | Anchor in inspected evidence | What to watch at normal speed, then frame-step if needed | Reject or leave unqualified when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Start/stop walk, run and tight left/right turns on flat paving | Actors in hill/gate/root views have different static poses; feet and transitions are not qualified | Foot planting through deceleration and turns; body orientation follows travel; knees/hips and armour deform coherently; contact shadow stays attached | Repeated skating during planted phases, sudden pose snaps, visible limb deformation, feet persistently floating/sinking; insufficient foot coverage means unqualified |
| Jump/land, then walk up/down the existing hill stairs and through West Gate | Working traversal must be preserved; existing stills cannot show landing/contact | Jump arc and landing response; feet meet treads; body and camera avoid geometry; input remains responsive on landing | Persistent penetration, missed surface contact, landing hitch/snap, blocked opening or stuck controller; a named-camera teleport cannot substitute for real input |
| Left-drag orbit/look and wheel zoom into/out of first person near tree and gate | Fixed views show bark/branch proximity but do not qualify camera behaviour | Smooth response and consistent framing; near clipping; self-body occlusion; readable horizon and HUD; camera recovery after collision | Camera cuts through solid trunk/wall, violent displacement, prolonged occlusion, jitter or unexpected mode changes |
| Stationary canopy hold, slow sideways pan, then walk toward/away from the tree | Native edges are cleaner than Editor; dense small foliage and triangular grass still read poorly | Leaf-edge crawl, bright speckle flicker, branch/leaf popping, changes in cluster coverage and shadow detail; distinguish wind motion from camera-induced instability | Repeated conspicuous shimmer/pops at normal speed, chunks vanish or change coverage abruptly, camera motion turns fine detail into flashing noise; lossy recording alone is insufficient evidence |
| Walk across the near-root shadow and pan from canopy to paving; repeat at another daylight angle if available | Static LOD1/baseline ties retain broad grounding, but fine dapple is unqualified | Root contact, branching cast silhouette, leaf holes, shadow movement corresponding to wind, abrupt cascade/detail changes | Detached contact, broad light leaks/new missing limbs, shadow silhouette switches or crawls during a continuous move; tiny unmatched wind differences alone do not establish a regression |

Use a continuous, ordinary player-camera route for movement tests. Keep full feet in frame for contact review and show at least a stationary lead-in/out for foliage and shadow clips. Label cuts explicitly; a montage should not conceal the transition under review. Prefer the same route, lighting and capture settings for candidate comparisons, with anonymous labels locked before mapping where feasible. Record changes in wind/actor poses rather than claiming exact geometric alignment.

For each clip, record: build/working-tree identity, scene, API/renderer and driver, GPU, actual viewport and render resolution/scale, AA and shadow settings, relevant leaf/caster variant, day/time, capture FPS/duration and encoding, and whether motion came from real input. Review at normal speed first. A passing item means no disqualifying fault observed in that sequence; it is not whole-game acceptance. When the clip does not show the relevant feet, transition or foliage scale, mark **unqualified**, not pass.

If the proposed private Xvfb/Zink route produces footage, label it **alternate diagnostic rendering route** and record the actual backend/device it reports. It can expose visible functional/animation faults under that route. Do not assign its results, timing or image stability to the baseline native OpenGL qualification by implication. Confirm baseline-relevant conclusions on the supported baseline route before qualification. Do not derive a p99 performance pass from a recorded video or its nominal FPS.

The current anonymous shadow frames contain a retained Development Console overlay from the harness camera-name error. Fresh motion footage should make such capture errors visible in its record and use a clean player for presentation review. Separate an actual gameplay/runtime defect from an erroneous capture command.
