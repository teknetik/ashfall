# Anonymous presentation protocol

The purpose is to judge the next native Ward candidate against its unchanged baseline and an actual AAA visual reference without seeing implementation labels. This is **anonymous presentation, not a genuinely blind experiment**: this critic already recognizes Ward and the official ARK images, and the subjects/HUD reveal identity. Record recognition with each verdict. Do not claim blinded evidence or statistical confidence from one reviewer.

1. Capture the current native Ward baseline and candidate at identical named-camera position, lens, time of day, exposure, native resolution, render scale, antialiasing and quality. Keep HUD and all current actors for gameplay qualification. Also take an identically configured HUD-hidden view for visual composition comparison, if supported; never substitute it for normal gameplay evidence.
2. Root assigns random opaque labels and left/right ordering to the two Ward images, stores the key outside the critic's report, and sends only the anonymous pair. Preserve original images and exact metadata. Display full frames fitted into equal-size panes without stretching, recoloring, sharpening, selective cropping or generated paintover. Full-resolution inspection remains available separately.
3. Critic records preference, recognition confidence, rubric scores and specific defects before root reveals the baseline/candidate mapping. “Tie” and “neither reaches the target” are valid outcomes. Require player-height views and motion samples for materials, canopy, contact, clipping and temporal claims.
4. Independently present the selected Ward native frame beside `ark-scorched-earth-official-courtyard.jpg`, matching view type as closely as available: player-height desert threshold, masonry and oasis foliage. ARK hardware/settings, lens, exposure and promotional capture processing remain unknown, so label these limits. Use both full images without claiming identical scene conditions or performance fairness.
5. Ask which image looks more convincing and why across construction, materials, lighting depth, density and silhouette; record identity recognition. Do not score human animation, physics or temporal stability from the ARK still. Strong preference for ARK means the AAA target remains unmet regardless of Ward's improvement over its own baseline.
6. Keep every rejected candidate and report. A better candidate can be retained while the overall verdict remains REVISE. Do not alter the rubric, repeatedly relabel identical images, or pressure reviewers to become “wowed” merely to terminate a loop.

The first reference-street/hill comparison is already recorded in `baseline-review.md`: ARK is preferred. A current native comparison remains pending until the desktop is available and matched captures exist.
