The character close-ups reveal an additional environment failure that wide screenshots conceal: the nearby West Gate pier has very soft, stretched-looking surface detail across large faces beside a much coarser adjacent wall/paving texture. The source of that mismatch is not diagnosed from pixels alone. The accepted gate's role/shape need not be replaced to investigate texel density, UVs and material response. The previously proposed bounded paving/threshold patch remains a useful separate environment task.
