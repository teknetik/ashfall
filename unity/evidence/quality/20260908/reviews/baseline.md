This review was performed by the separate `visual_critic` agent. It changed no game content, scripts, scene, Editor state or TODO checkboxes. The implementation agents' praise and polygon counts do not determine acceptance.

## Score rules

| Score | Meaning at the specified camera and intended play distance |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Missing, broken, or materially wrong; cannot assess the intended feature. |
| 1 | Obvious placeholder or severe defect dominates the view. |
| 2 | Recognizable and functional, but repeated defects prevent a convincing result. |
| 3 | Credible improvement; readily visible art or presentation weaknesses remain. |
| 4 | Strong at the intended distance, with only minor documented issues. |
| 5 | Meets the specific observed reference target in this view and tested motion/state. This is not “perfect everywhere.” |
| U | Unreviewed or evidence missing. Never substitute zero or a pass. |

Review each applicable dimension separately: composition/silhouette, construction/scale, material detail, lighting/depth, density/storytelling, character/animation, temporal stability and UI/readability. No averaging can hide a broken doorway, open branch join, malformed hand, intrusive collider or unreadable interaction. An item needs at least 4 in every applicable dimension **and** its evidence checks below; a reference-match claim needs a supported 5 in the claimed dimension. “Wowed” is not a reproducible test.
