The [retained original](../../../../../meshy/platform-terminals-20260908/README.md), detailed export and sampled LOD deviations are useful production evidence; small nearest-surface error does not prove a repaired screen, clean normals, stable mip/LOD appearance or good materials.

At the reported uniform 1.65 m height, width approximately 0.747 m and depth approximately 0.720 m, the family can plausibly fit the specified hill-platform envelope. Native capture must include the 1.8 m actor, all three bases, Linn's route and clear screen access. Preserve the accepted mission terminals and the separate travel landmarks.
