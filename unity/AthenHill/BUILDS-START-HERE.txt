  LinuxDevelopment/AthenHill.x86_64
  Development build of 5 October 2026, 04:27: everything below plus
  - MOUSE LOOK is always on: the mouse turns the camera with no button held; the pointer comes back whenever a window
    is open (pack, journal, a counter, pause) or while you hold LEFT ALT. LEFT MOUSE fires a drawn weapon (aiming or
    not), right mouse aims. Settings > Controls has look sensitivity and invert up/down.
  - HOTBAR: 1 = field rifle, 2 = scrap pistol; 3-0 hold the items you choose. In the field pack (Tab) there is a HOTBAR
    strip under the grid: drag a medkit, trauma kit, nano cell or surge pack onto 3-0, or select one and press its
    number. Drag a strip cell away or right-click it to clear it. Medkit on 3 and nano cell on 4 to start.
  - JOURNAL (J): your missions (city visit, Ossa's primer, each field order, A Roof in Ward), with T to track one. The
    tracked mission shows in Field Notes, on the compass (cyan diamond, waits at the edge when it is behind you) and
    as the marker over its target. The old Notes text is the journal's WARD NOTES tab.
  - YOUR HOME: once Ossa's primer is done, ask Linn on the hill "Is there anywhere in Ward I could stay?" — 40 credits
    leases the rust-red container on the south wall, behind the Nanofab yard. The door opens for you; E at the locker
    stores your loot (Store all salvage keeps medkits, gear and tools in your pack; Brann's bench uses parts from the
    locker once your pack runs short); E at the bunk sleeps to morning or evening, heals, saves, and Continue then
    wakes you there.
  - Ossa's locker and range lines are unvoiced for now (their old takes say "7"; ElevenLabs needs paying to redo them).
