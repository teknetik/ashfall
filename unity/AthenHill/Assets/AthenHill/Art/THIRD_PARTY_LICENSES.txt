# Third-party asset credits and licences

## Current music — user-supplied

Dust of the Giants and Dust of Alshain were supplied by the user on 8 September

## Athen Hill soundscape — ElevenLabs (effects and historical music)

"Hill at Dusk" instrumental music and twelve sound-effect assets were generated
for this project with ElevenLabs on 8 September 2026, using the project's account.
Music: music_v2. Sound effects: eleven_text_to_sound_v2. Original prompts describe
the colony setting and do not reference an existing soundtrack or artist.
The game uses edited, level-balanced versions with prepared loop boundaries.
Original responses, prompts and hashes are retained in
unity/staging/elevenlabs-audio/manifest.json and the adjacent source records.
Generated outputs are subject to the account's ElevenLabs terms.

## Ward NPC dialogue — AI generated voices

The spoken performances for Mira, Torr, Vex, Linn, Brann, Warden Ossa and
Warden Rell were generated with ElevenLabs text to speech in October 2026.
The performances use distinct British, American and Australian voice options.
All spoken lines match the authored on-screen dialogue. Voice IDs, source audio,
exact requests, provider response IDs and checksums are retained in
unity/staging/elevenlabs-audio/npc-voices/. The game ships edited offline clips;
it does not send dialogue or player data to ElevenLabs at runtime.

These notices cover the sources used to author Athen Hill's replacement
colonist. Keep this notice with distributions containing the derived assets.
Original source files, pack metadata and file hashes are retained in the
repository under `blender/sources/`.
