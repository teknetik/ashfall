# Athen Hill

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Native Linux game, Unity 6000.6 UI Toolkit. The historical browser game is retired.

## Product Purpose

A playable original homage to a 2001 science-fiction MMO colony city. Explore the hill, talk to four colonists, trade supplies, and connect to the sector lattice.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Third-person keyboard/mouse play, six hotbar actions, four branching conversations, buy/sell, inventory, notes, pause, audio and reduced-motion settings. Keep the saved Unity scene, editable UI assets, NPC roots/routes, and gameplay semantics. Target 60 fps at 1920×1080.

## Brand Commitments

Original Free Column identity and original assets. The user's 8 September 2026 reference image sets the UI direction: worn metal frames, inset dark surfaces, illustrated equipment, cyan state indicators, compact corner HUD, open city view.

## Evidence on Hand

UI source: AthenHill/Assets/AthenHill/UI. Gameplay data: AthenHill/Assets/AthenHill/Data. Native keyboard verification: tools/city_loop_check.py. Reference preserved under ../refs/ui_20260908/.

## Product Principles

Keep the city visible. Make actions readable by mouse and keyboard. UI is ordinary editable Unity content. Do not imply multiplayer chat or unimplemented game systems.
