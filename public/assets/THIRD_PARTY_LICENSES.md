# Third-party asset credits and licences

These notices cover the sources used to author Athen Hill's replacement
colonist. Keep this notice with distributions containing the derived assets.
Original source files, pack metadata and file hashes are retained in the
repository under `blender/sources/`.

## Elvaerwyn — Grump hair

**Grump hair (`elvs_grump_hair`) by Elvaerwyn, used under Creative Commons
Attribution (CC-BY).**

- [Original asset entry](https://www.makehumancommunity.org/node/2796)
- [Official Hair 02 pack and licence listing](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/hair02.html)
- [Creative Commons licence information](https://creativecommons.org/share-your-work/cclicenses/)

Changes for Athen Hill: fitted to the colonist's head and rig, reduced the
raised crest, changed the colour and material response, reduced mesh density
in Blender, and prepared the result for glTF. The original source header and
pack manifest specify `CC-BY` without a version; this notice preserves that
designation. No creator endorsement is implied.

## MakeHuman Community / MPFB

Human anatomy, morph targets, rig and weights, `middleage_caucasian_male`
skin, low-poly eyes and their materials, and eyebrow001 cards are MakeHuman
Community graphical assets under **CC0 1.0 Universal**. They were imported
using MPFB 2.0.17, fitted and adapted for the colonist. The graphical-asset
licence is separate from the MPFB add-on's GPLv3 source-code licence.

## grinsegold — Beard Sigmund WIP

**Beard Sigmund WIP by grinsegold**, fitted and recoloured for Athen Hill.
The current official [Bodyparts 05 pack](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/bodyparts05.html)
and its retained per-asset manifest declare **CC0**.
[Original asset entry](https://www.makehumancommunity.org/node/877).

The original `.mhclo` file also contains a legacy MakeClothes-generated
`AGPL3` header. That header is retained unchanged alongside the current CC0
pack metadata. The project's sourcing basis is the current official CC0
declaration; the discrepant historical notice is preserved here explicitly.

## Mindfront — Aksel skin normal map

**Aksel skin by Mindfront**, **CC0** in the official
[Skins 02 pack](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/skins02.html).
[Original asset entry](https://www.makehumancommunity.org/node/850).

Only `Aksel_Skin_NRM.png` is used in the selected skin material, at a reduced
normal strength of 0.22. The Aksel diffuse albedo was rejected; the selected
diffuse skin is the MakeHuman system skin credited above.

## Poly Haven texture contributors

- [Blue Metal Plate](https://polyhaven.com/a/blue_metal_plate): normal detail
  and diffuse-derived neutral wear for the olive armour. A source roughness
  map is retained, although the selected metal material uses constant roughness.
- [Fabric Leather 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_leather_01): normal and
  roughness detail for dark suit and gear materials.

Both are **CC0**, as stated in [Poly Haven's asset licence](https://polyhaven.com/license).
The project adjusts UV scale, normal strength and material response; the
metal diffuse is adapted into a neutral wear texture using Blender. Original
downloaded maps are preserved in the source archive.

## Retained audition sources

RehmanPolanski's [Viking beard and moustache](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/bodyparts05.html)
are CC0 and remain in the authoring source archive. They were auditioned and
replaced by the Sigmund beard in the selected character. No paid model is
included. Athen Hill's armour geometry and faction markings are original
project work; the character is not an extracted commercial-game asset.
