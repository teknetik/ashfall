# Fetra 2436 workshop trolley | marso by M-XR

Isaac Sim/PhysX asset. Independent acceptance and final release inputs are recorded outside this package. No NVIDIA external sign-off is implied.

Reference `workshop_trolley_fetra_2436.usda` into a clean Z-up, metre-scale stage at any prim path. Select the root `PhysX` variant `Enabled` before play. Enable the standard Isaac extensions `omni.graph.scriptnode` and `isaacsim.core.nodes` and permit the embedded ScriptNode. This package was exercised in Isaac Sim 6.1.0-rc.26+mr.0.7c206f75.local; other runtimes are unqualified. A graph-disabled import is not the supported interactive configuration.

The default prim is `/Trolley`; references remap it under the chosen instance root. Eleven rigid links live under `Asset/link_*`; joints under `Joints`. Keep the root movable and give it a rigid floor. No authoring helper, absolute path, fixture or test prop is needed to operate the asset. The embedded graph resolves its own instance path.

Press either brake pedal approximately18 degrees with a physical toe/point force. At13 degrees it latches both the associated wheel and caster swivel at their current orientations, without a directional alignment lock. Lift below5 degrees to release. Wheels2 and3 use fixed forks; wheels0 and1 have swivels. Use the left push handle to move and steer, stop before parking, and press both pedals for storage access.

Set the root Boolean `trolley:drawerLocked` or `trolley:doorLocked` false to represent unlocking with the omitted key. Pull the drawer toward local-Y through up to0.39m; open the cupboard about+Z through up to105 degrees. Close physically, then set the corresponding Boolean true. A lock request engages only when the drawer is within3mm or door within1 degree of seated; it does not teleport or remotely close a part. `...LockedEffective` reports actual engagement. The key/lock internals and robot frictional grasp are not modeled. The shelves are fixed at the delivered positions; shelf adjustment is unsupported.

Supported evaluation is single-instance, finite-force handling of storage, wheel/pedal operation and low-speed transport on a rigid workshop floor, using small rigid payloads. Proof uses2kg in the drawer,2kg on a cupboard shelf and5kg on the top. These are tested examples, not a safe-load certification. The manufacturer's400kg rating is provenance and has not been qualified in this model. No humans, liquids, flexible contents or high-speed towing are covered. Two-copy behavior remains explicitly not tested at the user's deferral.

Total unloaded mass66kg comes from manufacturer data. Link allocations and COM/inertia use signed mesh-volume moments and material density weights normalized to the stated link masses. Contact coefficients, restitution, lock compliance and brake torque are research-informed estimates, not physical-sample calibration. Tyre friction static/dynamic is0.65/0.50, coated steel0.35/0.25 and wood0.40/0.30 with average combine. The floor in proof is0.50/0.40. `mechanism.json` retains the local mass/inertia estimates. Wheel rolling, sliding and tipping on a ramp must be distinguished; onset of rolling is not a measured friction coefficient.

The accepted textured handoff is preserved geometrically:53 visual meshes, topology, transforms, normals, UVs, material bindings and wood-edge subsets. All14 original maps remain. The exposed-metal raw linear basecolor has one downstream sRGB-encoded derivative with matching color-space declaration for NVIDIA conformance; maximum decoded linear RGB error0.0030. No finish redesign is included. Neutral clay catalogue imagery is a presentation override only.

Control interface and generic benchmark scope

Direct joint-drive targets are not a supported user control interface. The embedded controller owns joint drive gains, force caps and position/velocity targets on every physics step, so external targets may be overwritten. Operate through physical forces at handles/pedals and the documented virtual-key attributes. Official generic joint_movement result: fail. Recorded watchdog timeouts are incomplete evidence and establish neither a joint-specific failure nor a pass; they do not prove physical impossibility. Completed everyday point-force operations are a separate positive claim. No general direct-drive compatibility is claimed.
