The script writes six channel sets under `candidate-v1/`: `CoatedSteel`, `ShutterSteel`, and four door projection layers. Channels are BaseColor, GL tangent Normal, Roughness, Metallic, Opacity and WearMask. They are explicit 16-bit PNG files, with sRGB encoding only for BaseColor. No view transform or lighting is baked into the map encoder. Original provider maps are hashed and retained. A future URP packed derivative must put semantic metallic in R and one-minus-roughness in A; do not bind ARM as metallic/smoothness.

The quiet coating uses the inspected Blue Metal Plate crop at its actual 2.5 m source scale. Translation-only overlap blending assembles a repeatable field without copying its photographed panel seams, mirroring normal axes, stretching the crop, or applying a nonlinear warp. The crop covers 0.9375 × 1.25 m; the 0.8 × 1 m quilt periods both divide the existing 4 m output tile. The overlap widths are 0.1375 × 0.25 m. This can still reveal repeated scratch arrangements or softened overlap strips: reject those in the source preview instead of calling the quilt automatically acceptable. Blue pigment is selectively shifted toward neutral charcoal while photographic fine variation survives. The source roughness stays unchanged on quiet paint; its median is about 0.28, so this is deliberately a semi-gloss control audition. Do not claim it already matches the reference’s dry coating.

Oxide microstructure is sampled from retained Rusty Metal Sheet at its true 2 m scale, with matched roughness/normal. It contributes only inside physically authored wear. Sparse, millimetre-width coating losses occupy selected shutter lips; rows use different short segments, with no wholesale rust stripes. Additional small abrasions register to the two real lift handles at world Z −8.45/−9.55, Y 1.32. Intact coating and oxide remain dielectric. Explicitly authored thin bare-steel scratches have their own metallic and roughness response. A weak lower-contact dust layer has a separate dry response; nothing globally forces all surfaces to one roughness.

Door registration is the major limit. `installed-registration.json` records 32 source meshes, current mesh/meta hashes, matching source UV/index arrays, fitted source-world geometry, and per-object front UV offsets. The four-metre density is correct, but every primitive has a different offset. Field leaf/inset handle-region samples additionally alias bevel/side surfaces; front/back reuse is unresolved on both buildings. Therefore **do not put unique handle wear in the common tiled Lit material**, and do not use the suggested front-offset values as permission to do so. Their original names are diagnostic, not an installation contract. No UV0 or source geometry is changed.

The four door layer sets instead use measured whole-leaf bounds and actual handle/hinge positions. Their U axis follows +Unity world Z, V follows +Unity world Y. Opacity is unpremultiplied coverage, separate from colour, normal, roughness and metallic. A future implementation needs a front-facing, depth-limited projection/decal restricted to the listed coated receiver paths; bronze handles, kickplates, seals, the back face, surrounding stone and other doors must not receive it. The source preview uses an additional inspection-only UV layer and a front-normal gate, keeping retained UV0 intact. Because the existing door UV0 U axis follows −Z, the preview flips only the projected normal’s tangent X before combining it with that UV0 basis. The exported projection normal retains its declared +Z/+Y basis. Exporting the inspection meshes is not part of this proposal.
