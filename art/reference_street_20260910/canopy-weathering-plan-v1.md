# Finery canopy material audition

The native03 critic passed construction at 4 and retained the repaired pocket/hem join, but scored cloth material 3: smooth red coating, plain underside, clean repair rectangles and weak weave. Preserve all V3 geometry, rigid supports and source files. This is a material audition, not an accepted result.

`author_canopy_weathering_v1.py` is staged for **live Blender MCP** after the generator author hands back exclusive access. It has not executed Blender yet. Its independent studio never overwrites the generator scene or the prior canopy files.

The proposed macro maps separate the 4.58×6.5m membrane from the 0.3×6.5m hem chart. Raised seam traces, the free hem and both rotated repair boundaries use the exact V3 source functions and existing UV offsets. Sun bleaching is interrupted by fibre-scale variation; sediment is limited to short runs below the attachment/pocket. The repair cloth has a different restrained dye lot. Diffuse bakes exclude scene illumination. Roughness follows dye wear and deposits; metal remains zero.

The original CC0 [Book Pattern](https://polyhaven.com/a/book_pattern) maps are retained with API MD5/size and SHA256 under `refs/reference-street/20260910/cloth-candidates/book_pattern`. Its recorded physical tile is 0.3m. The plain weave is a better starting surface than the current coarse checked towel normal. A compressed grayscale version of the diffuse supplies independent fine albedo; the original GL normal remains byte-identical. Source inspection saw fine yarn/slub variation without a large copied pattern. Opposed-light material review must still check residual fixed shading; the web preview is not evidence of the final material. CC0 rights: https://polyhaven.com/license.

Installed UV0 is fabric metres divided by 0.4 and stays unchanged. For each macro map with dimensions M, use BaseMap ST=0.4/M; detail ST=M/0.3. URP17.6 LitInput.hlsl applies detail ST after BaseMap ST, yielding original metres/0.3. Detail albedo is centred near linear 0.5, matching URP's multiply convention; it must be imported as sRGB so the shader samples that intended linear signal. Base-color maps are sRGB; independent roughness is linear and packs into Unity alpha as 1−roughness. No alpha clipping, displacement or strong baked lights are introduced.

Bake one explicit family/channel per call. Retain partial/completed outputs; a rerun may not overwrite them. Then append only the canopy V3 source scene into a light review session, copy its materials, bind the actual baked maps with the same Unity transforms/tints, and render top/underside/hem plus both repair boundaries under two opposed light directions. No Unity installation until source review. Recheck identical native noon/hour16 cameras and continuous proximity motion after installation. Fine maps need mip and anisotropic review rather than a no-mipmap sharpness trick.

The provisional normal strength 0.48 and detail-albedo strength 0.6 are audition values. They are not quality claims. All preserved supports, mesh buffers, collider data, actor roles/routes, scene lighting and prior materials must be recorded before any Unity material reassignment and render-chunk rebuild.
