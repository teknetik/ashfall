Scene and composition: a dignified, important public building commanding its modest working desert-town square. Three-quarter view from the plaza at human eye height, about 1.7 m, looking toward the front and one side. Architecture: believable buildable modular architecture, symmetrical principal facade, 12 m wide, 8 m deep, about 13 m to the parapet, with a slim mast reaching about 17 m. Two monumental storeys, carefully proportioned in metres, vertical civic presence. Massive warm sandstone ashlar and poured mineral concrete from a pre-collapse corporate civic building, patiently maintained across generations using salvaged steel and bronze. Coherent structural load paths, convincing block courses and mortar joints, real wall thickness, modular repeated piers/windows/cornice units.

Ground storey: a deep recessed civic portal with a clear opening 3.2 m wide and 4.2 m tall. Heavy riveted weathered steel/bronze double doors set far inside a stepped sandstone surround beneath a thick lintel. Exactly three broad sandstone approach steps. Slightly battered, inward-sloping sandstone corner piers. Small deeply recessed barred windows on both sides of the entrance. Industrial wall lamps flanking the portal.

Believable close-view detail: a surface-mounted conduit run and junction box, a few riveted steel repair patches integrated into repaired areas, chipped block arrises especially at corners, sand settled on ledges and in joints. Distinct rough sandstone, dense poured mineral concrete, oxidized bronze, weathered steel, and woven cloth. Varied warm stone block tones and real-scale grain. Wear is concentrated at touch points, edges, drips and repairs; maintained large surfaces retain material clarity, not uniformly dirty. Crisp bevels, intentional joints, recessed openings and robust architectural silhouette.
