Subject: VANGUARD HALL, the civic hall and Warden muster house of Ward, the last oasis city on the desert planet Tir. It is a pre-collapse corporate-era civic building of massive warm sandstone ashlar and poured mineral concrete, patiently repaired over generations with salvaged steel and bronze. It must feel like a real, dignified, lived-in building, the most important public building on the square.

Architecture (keep it buildable as a modular real-time game asset, symmetrical, 12 m wide, about 13 m to the parapet, 8 m deep):
- Ground storey: a deep recessed civic portal, 3.2 m wide and 4.2 m tall, with heavy riveted weathered steel/bronze double doors inside a stepped sandstone surround with a thick lintel; three broad sandstone steps up to it; slightly battered (inward-sloping) sandstone corner piers; small deep-set barred windows either side.
- Above the portal: a cast metal nameplate with raised letters reading exactly VANGUARD HALL.
- Upper storey: a row of five tall narrow recessed windows behind vertical weathered-steel sun louvres, separated by sandstone pilasters; a long red woven Warden banner with an abstract pale twin-stripe emblem hanging from brackets on the central pilaster.
- Deep projecting stone cornice with drainage spouts and dark water/dust staining below them, a stepped parapet, a small central stepped attic, and a slim steel comms mast with a crossbar and a small dish rising to about 17 m.
- Believable detail: wall-mounted industrial lamps each side of the portal, a surface conduit run, a junction box, a few riveted steel repair patches, chipped block arrises at corners, sand gathered on ledges and in joints. Wear concentrated at edges, drips and repairs, not uniform grime.

Style: contemporary AAA open-world game environment art (photoreal PBR materials, real stone block courses with varied tone, crisp geometry, strong silhouette), golden late-afternoon desert light with cool sky-lit shadows, a three-quarter view from the plaza at human eye height, a person-sized figure near the steps for scale. No text other than VANGUARD HALL. No fantasy castle ornament, no modern glass curtain wall, no logos of real companies.
