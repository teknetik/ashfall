# tutor: Brann walks the player through the first hand-made job

Steady Hands bench programme, `tutor` agent, 21 November 2026. Carl: "Is there a tutorial for it. Maybe Brann walking you
through the process as this will be a players first craft"; on cost: "You can use all eleven lab credits."

Status: **installed and verified with `-nographics`; Edit Mode tests written; nobody has heard the lines in the game or seen
the prompt.** The native run is the orchestrator's.

## What it does

- **Per step.** When a step becomes the next useful thing (the session's `HintAction`, the same thing the idle hints point at),
  Brann says its line from his counter through the bench's own voice (`BenchVoice.SayLine`: his talk clip, his voice, the
  caption in the bark strip). One extra line at most inside a row, for a repeat that needs it (the vial, the screw dish,
  the tap on the seized screw, the pin pull, the strap strip).
- **Per kind of action.** The first time each teach key comes up (the seven archetypes; Point and Window in two forms each:
  `seat`, `turn`, `point_aim`, `point`, `flick`, `path`, `window_strike`, `window_hold`, `wipe`): a how-line ("Hold on it and
  circle the mouse slow. Feel it bite."), a small prompt by the work with the mouse and keyboard forms (stays until an action
  of that kind has been done once), and the ghost at once (`session.Hint(3)`).
- **Taper.** Stages 0 and 1: full lines with the reason. Stages 2 to 4: short lines. Stages 5 and 6: silent unless the player
  stalls (15 s idle) or makes a mistake. Stage 7: silent (Brann's existing lines). A stall in stages 0 to 4 (20 s) says the
  step's line once more. Recovery steps have their own lines in every stage up to 6.
- **Never talks over Brann.** The tutor asks for a line only when Brann is silent with nothing of the bench's queued
  (`BenchVoice.Busy`), after 0.5 s of quiet, and once the next thing has stayed the same for 0.6 s (a quick hand is not talked
  over). Order: welcome, recovery, step, extra, how-line. A mistake drops the tutor's waiting step lines (Brann's fault line
  speaks). Lines for a step that is no longer next are dropped. It never takes the hand and blocks nothing.
- **When.** On until the player has **finished a job at the bench by hand** (`CraftingModel.FinishedAtBench`, set by
  `CompleteAssembly`, saved additively as `CraftingState.benchFinished`; null in older saves). So every existing save is guided,
  including Carl's (his made the grip on the timer before the bench existed); a second bench job after a finished one is not;
  a detached QA job is. Settings → Controls → **Brann's guidance** (default on, `PlayerPrefs`
  `AthenHill.Settings.v1.BenchGuidance`); switching it mid-job silences or resumes at once and removes the instruction line.
- **Resume.** What was said and which kinds of action were done are kept in `AssemblyJob.Notes["tutor"]`
  (`said=T|T;done=K,K`), inside the bench blob the save already carries (`WardSaveData.benchJob`, CONTRACT.md B): no save shape
  change. A resumed job does not repeat anything and gives no welcome. Reminders are not kept.

| Setting | Start | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `voicedStages` | 4 | Step lines are offered in stages 0 to this |
| `lastGuidedStage` | 6 | Nothing at all after this stage (stage 7 keeps Brann's lines) |
| `stallSeconds` | 20 s | Idle in stages 0-4 before the step's line is said again (once per step and sitting) |
| `quietStallSeconds` | 15 s | Idle in stages 5-6 before the step's line is said |
| `settleSeconds` | 0.6 s | The next thing must hold this long before its line starts |
| `gapSeconds` | 0.5 s | Quiet after Brann stops before the tutor speaks |
| `maxPending` | 4 | Lines waiting at most (the lowest kind goes first) |

(top 160 px) and above the way-out line (70 px from the bottom).

## Departures

1. **Point and Window are taught in two forms each** (aim / mark, strike / hold): the motions differ too much for one line.
   The press held in its band (`sustain`) and the cure's wait are explained by their step lines, not a how-line.
2. **The ghost at once uses `session.Hint(3)`**, which also raises the session's hint level to 3: the bench's own 10 s glint and
   25 s line then wait for progress or a fault on that action. The tutor's 20 s stall line covers the gap.
3. **Stage 7 has no tutor lines**, also no how-line: a kind of action first met there would not be taught (none is: Flick is
   met at `s3.spin` and the optional `s0.shake_before`).
4. **The welcome line** ("First one's by hand. I'll talk you through it.") is extra to the brief's list; it plays after
   Brann's own `take_bench` on a fresh job only.
5. **The prompt has its own small stylesheet** (`BenchTutor.uss`, the bench legend's palette and bronze edge) rather than
   classes added to bench's `AssemblyHud.uss`.
6. **A detached QA job is treated as a first job**, so the native check sees the tutor.
