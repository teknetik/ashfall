A reference sheet for 3D modelling of the worked materials for a hand-made pistol grip, salvaged in a desert colony. Six separate groups laid out on a flat neutral grey background in two rows of three, each group seen from a three-quarter view above, photographic studio product render, soft even light, no labels, no text captions, no hands, no tools. Everything is worn, salvaged, lightly dusty and physically plausible at real size.

1. Two salvaged scrap alloy sheets, each about 120 x 90 mm and 2.5 mm thick: aluminium alloy panel offcuts with old chipped industrial orange paint on one face, a fragment of white stencilled lettering, two old bolt holes, one torn ragged edge and scratches; on one of them a bright scribed outline of a pistol grip side plate.
2. The same sheet cut up: two matching pistol-grip side plates about 95 x 42 mm (a rounded parallelogram) with filed bright edges and four small drilled screw holes each; a narrow strap strip about 110 x 12 mm lying flat, and the same strip bent in two places into a shallow U; several jagged offcuts; a few curled swarf spirals from drilling.
3. Thin electrical leads, 1.6 mm thick, in stages side by side: one as cut with a red marker band; one trimmed shorter; one with 8 mm of insulation stripped and the copper strands twisted; one with the bare end tinned bright silver with solder. Beside them a tiny axial trim resistor with colour bands and two wire legs, two short black heat-shrink sleeves, and the finished damper loop: two leads soldered across the resistor with the two sleeves shrunk tight over the joints.
4. A small stoppered glass vial about 70 mm tall, two-thirds full of dull grey metallic fluid, with a scratched level line on the glass and a wired-on rubber stopper.
5. A small steel-and-wood wire bobbin about 35 mm across shown twice: empty, and wound full of bright enamelled copper wire.
6. A freshly fused pistol-grip core as it leaves a press: a dull grey cast alloy spine about 95 mm long with a round bearing pocket in its side, still hot with a faint dull-red heat tint and blueing, thin ragged flash around its parting line and a stubby sprue still attached.
