# Ward utility lamps — 8 September 2026

Original construction for the accepted Ward courtyard direction. This replaces the five primitive avenue lamp visuals and Finery's four-piece entrance fitting. It preserves their placements, the fifteen existing avenue collider proxies, the Finery facade and all travel/interaction routes.

The family uses a sand-darkened cast foot, bolted galvanised anchor plate, tapered painted steel mast, gasketed service hatch, restrained bronze hardware, actual curved supply conduit, an enamel rain hood, socket, opal glass light capsule and protective cage. A compact wall variant uses the same serviceable light head on a bolted triangular bracket with a wall feed. Parts are original Blender construction in metres; no generated lettering, copied game assets or artificial subdivision.

The post's ground footprint is 0.46 m square inside the existing 0.48 m collider. The mast is 0.166 m wide inside the existing 0.18 m post collider. Lamp hardware projects only above 4.1 m. The wall plate mounts at Finery's existing entrance fixture, at 3.51 m, so its lower edge clears the carved lintel at 3.13 m. Hardware and surface scale must be reviewed at arm's length, from the route, at dusk and at night.

Material regions stay distinct: dielectric painted steel, exposed steel fasteners, oxidised bronze fittings, rubber gaskets and opal glass. Original procedural material maps provide fine surface variation without baked directional light. Dirt is concentrated in the base/collars and rain hood; ordinary metal surfaces remain readable. Source texture dimensions are retained and metre-scale UVs prevent a single image being stretched along the whole post.

Local lights are authored at full night output and bound to CityLightCircuit with matching LampEmission material identity. Their numerical intensity is an audition value, not a measured lighting acceptance. The native night pass must check pavement pools, face readability, source position, shadow acne, falloff, transitions and cost. No native visual or frame-time acceptance is claimed by the source/export step.

Audited avenue base centres, in Unity X/Z metres: (35,-5), (35,5), (12,-25), (-12,25), (-32,5). Finery wall mount is (16.801,3.51,-18), facing west against the existing wall front at x=16.80. Existing originals remain in place with only renderers disabled when the scoped installer runs.
