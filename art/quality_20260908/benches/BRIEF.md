# Two plaza benches — source production, 9 September 2026

This bounded pass replaces only the two three-cube bench sets under `AuthoredWorld/AAA Environment Dressing`: the hill bench at (-3.4, 1.5, -5.8) and the avenue bench at (11.5, 0, 5.7). The four separate `PROP_bench_00–03` groups and their collider proxies are outside this installation. They remain open in the broader TODO.

The first source audit recorded a 2.1 m long seat with a 0.48 m depth and an oversized 0.575 m seat top above local ground. Neither plaza bench had collision. The replacement retains the centres and long axes, uses a 2.08 m seat with 0.474 m depth and approximately 0.45 m seat top, plus a gently reclined back, rounded timber armrests, bolted steel legs, load-transfer members, bracing, shoe plates and fixings. No sitting mechanic is inferred.

All geometry is original Blender construction in metres. Board grain follows each board's long axis. Photographic materials retain their full original resolution. The initial 0.5 m Rough Wood scan repeated large cracks too often along the bench; its audition remains under `revision-01`. Revision 02 uses an individual-board region of [Poly Haven Wooden Planks](https://polyhaven.com/a/wooden_planks), a 2 m CC0 scan by Charlotte Baglioni (photography) and Dario Barresi (processing). It avoids photographed board gaps and repeated quarter-length damage. Original photo maps are retained under `refs/quality_20260908/benches/wooden_planks`; metallic is zero and smoothness is packed as one minus source roughness. End grain and metal/rubber materials are original project work.

The source review also caught a rear arm support ending above its load-transfer member, back supports passing through the timber, smeared arm-side UVs, and a lower long tie without a convincing connection to the side frame. Revision 02 corrects those defects with proper support junctions, welded shoe sockets, side rails and measured board-interior UV strips. The intermediate UV audition is retained under `revision-02-uv-audition`; its photographed plank gaps are rejected. Inspection and buffer validation follow the corrected export. These source changes are not independent visual or native acceptance.

The installer adds separate conservative colliders following the seat, back, arms, legs and feet. Grounding and end clearance must be tested with real input in Unity. The back projects approximately 0.11 m farther behind the original primitive seat; review the actual hill and avenue routes before acceptance. No runtime geometry assembly or physics system is introduced.
